About the Festival

WPU StreamFeast 2026 is a three-day music festival celebrating the best of Indonesian music. Join us for an unforgettable experience filled with live performances, food, and fun activities.

The festival will feature a diverse lineup of talented Indonesian artists across various genres, ensuring there's something for everyone.
